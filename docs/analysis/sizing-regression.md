## Reading the results

- **App generally quotes fewer vehicles**, most on **single-shift schedules** — the legacy flat 3:1 charge adder is applied unconditionally, while the app credits free overnight/weekend charging (a single shift leaves 16 idle hours). This is the largest, most defensible divergence.
- **Divergence grows with system size** (Large ≫ Small): the legacy per-stage ceilings + flat multipliers compound, so absolute Δ scales with fleet size.
- **`utilization`-bound systems diverge most**: when the app finds charging free (headroom is the only constraint, ×1.25), the legacy ÷0.75×1.20 stack (~1.6×) roughly doubles the gap.
- **Exception — E7 (ebase7)**: the only vehicle where the app tends to quote MORE. Its slow charger + small battery push the app's rotation availability (A_cap) below the legacy flat 0.75, so the app sizes UP where the legacy rule under-charged. A real modeling difference, not a bug — worth confirming against the workbook.
- **Caveat**: legacy = the stated hand rule, not the real workbook. Numbers move when the authoritative formulas replace `legacySizing.ts`; the harness and analysis are unchanged.
